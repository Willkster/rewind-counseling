What it is like here

A small practice, on purpose

Trauma is the work

Not a specialty tucked beside a general caseload. EMDR, ILF neurofeedback, play therapy and the Gottman Method are all in use here, every week.

Unhurried by design

The practice was built around the idea that healing from trauma shouldn't feel clinical or rushed. That applies to how clinicians work, not just to clients.

Actual colleagues

Five clinicians in one Kailua office, with a physical therapist involved in intensive work. Small enough that you know everyone's cases.

For licensed clinicians

Working here

What we look for

  • An active Hawaii license, or license eligibility, as an LCSW, LMFT, or comparable
  • Genuine interest in trauma work, and the patience it asks for
  • Training in EMDR, play therapy, neurofeedback or the Gottman Method is welcome, and we will support you in adding one
  • Comfort being one of a handful of people rather than a name on a large roster

Current openings

Openings to be confirmed

We are glad to hear from clinicians whose work fits the practice even when nothing is formally posted. If the fit is right, we would rather know you exist than miss you because the timing was off.

Send us a note

Practical details such as employment type, caseload expectations, and compensation are to be confirmed and are best discussed directly. Ask us early rather than late.

For students and supervisees

Supervision

Supervision is its own skill, and a bad match can shape a whole early career. Here is what we can offer and what we cannot, stated plainly.

Play therapy supervision

Availabletoward the RPT credential

Who supervises
Rachel Whipple, LCSW, RPT-S. As a Registered Play Therapist Supervisor, Rachel provides Play Therapy Supervision for clinicians working toward their own RPT credential.
Who it suits
Clinicians who are already licensed, or working toward licensure, and who want play therapy to be a real part of their practice rather than an occasional tool.
Format and fee
To be confirmed, including whether supervision runs individually or in a group, how often you would meet, and the fee.
Ask about play therapy supervision

Supervision toward licensure

TBCavailability to be confirmed

What this covers
The supervised clinical hours Hawaii requires on the way to an LCSW or LMFT. This is separate from play therapy supervision, which leads to a different credential.

To be confirmed, including which of our clinicians is approved and available to supervise toward licensure, how many supervisees they can take, and the fee. Ask and we will tell you honestly whether we have room.
