Common questions

Before your first visit

What is EMDR?

Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing. It is a structured, well researched therapy for trauma, and it is also used for performance anxiety. Several of our clinicians hold specialized EMDR training.

What is ILF Neurofeedback?

Infra-Low Frequency neurofeedback is a non-invasive approach that helps calm the nervous system. Your clinician assesses what you are experiencing, then builds a protocol specific to your symptoms. It works well alongside talk therapy rather than replacing it.

What ages do you work with?

It depends on the clinician. Rachel Whipple works with adolescents and adults age 14 and up. Janina Martin works with youth 15 and up and adults across the lifespan. Others work with individuals and families across the lifespan.
