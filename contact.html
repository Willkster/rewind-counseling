Before you come in

Your first visit

Booking a first appointment is the hardest part for most people. Here is what actually happens, so there are fewer unknowns.

You book

Through the secure client portal, linked from any Book button on this site. You can request a specific clinician, or leave it to us.

We match you

If you have not picked someone, we suggest the clinician whose focus fits what you have described.

You come in

407 Uluniu St, Suite 100. The first session is mostly you telling us what is going on, at whatever pace you want.

You decide

No obligation to continue with the same person, or at all. If a different clinician fits better, say so and we will move you.

Book an appointment Read the bios first

If you are in crisis, do not wait for an appointment. Call or text 988 for the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, available 24 hours a day. In an emergency, call 911. This practice is not a crisis service and the inbox is not monitored around the clock.

A note on email: please keep anything you send us brief and non-clinical. Email is not a secure way to share medical details. Anything sensitive is better raised through the client portal or in session.
